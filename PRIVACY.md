# Privacy Policy for OshiReader+

Effective date: June 7, 2026

OshiReader+ is an iOS app for following favorite creators, idols, topics, and related public web content. This policy explains what information the app stores, sends, and uses to provide its features.

## Information Stored on Your Device

OshiReader+ stores app data locally on your device so the app can remember your preferences and reading state. This may include:

- Watch keywords and aliases you add
- Feed items and saved pages
- Custom URLs and source ordering
- Hidden items
- Display settings, wallpaper choices, and avatar compositions
- Cached article HTML for offline reading
- Optional admin or service configuration values you enter in the app

You can remove local app data from the Settings screen by using the clear-data control, or by deleting the app from your device.

## Information Sent for App Functionality

OshiReader+ sends limited information needed to provide its core features:

- Watch keywords, aliases, notification preferences, and collection settings may be sent to the configured OshiReader backend so feeds can be collected and synced.
- Search terms, translation text, public article URLs, and public feed requests may be sent to the backend or relevant public content providers when you use search, translation, feed, reader, or avatar features.
- Your Apple Push Notification service device token, notification environment, device identifier, and per-install device secret may be sent to the backend so OshiReader+ can authenticate watch-term synchronization and device-scoped background refresh requests. If you allow notifications, the same registration is also used to send keyword alerts.
- If you use app features that retrieve public content, the websites and services you request may receive normal network information such as your IP address and request metadata.

The app does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect your name, email address, precise location, contacts, health data, camera data, microphone data, or payment information.

## Public Content and Backend Data

The OshiReader backend collects public posts, articles, media metadata, thumbnails, titles, authors, URLs, and related text from supported public sources to create matching feed items for your watch terms. This content is used to provide the feed, saved-item, reader, notification, and search experiences.

## Notifications

Notifications are optional. OshiReader+ may register an APNs token with the backend for device authentication and device-scoped background refresh; granting notification permission allows that same registration to send visible alerts when new matching feed items are found. You can disable notifications in the app or in iOS Settings.

## Photos Permission

OshiReader+ asks for Photos permission only when you choose to save an image from the reader to your Photos library. The app does not scan or upload your photo library.

## Tracking and Advertising

OshiReader+ does not use advertising identifiers, App Tracking Transparency, third-party advertising SDKs, or data broker tracking. Data is used to provide app functionality and is not sold.

## Data Retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or remove the app. Backend data used for app functionality, such as watch terms, matching feed records, and notification tokens, is retained as needed to operate the service unless it is removed through app functionality or an administrative request.

## Security

OshiReader+ uses standard Apple platform networking protections for HTTPS connections where supported. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but the app is designed to limit collected information to what is needed for its features.

## Children

OshiReader+ is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as OshiReader+ changes. Updates will be posted in this repository with a revised effective date.
