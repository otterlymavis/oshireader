OshiReader+ is a native feed reader designed to help you follow your favorite Japanese creators, idols, anime, and topics in one unified timeline.

Track your "Oshi" across supported Japanese media platforms and curated news feeds. OshiReader+ searches public feeds and news sources to bring you the latest articles, videos, and discussions without algorithmic clutter.

KEY FEATURES:
• Multi-Source Ingestion: Search and follow topics across YouTube, NicoNico, TVer, note, Ameblo, Natalie, BARKS, ModelPress, Yahoo News, and curated Japanese entertainment RSS feeds.
• Alias Support: Add nicknames and alternative spellings to ensure you never miss an update.
• Clean & Focused Reader: Built-in distraction-free article reader with image saving and quick sharing.
• On-Device Privacy: All feed ingestion and data storage happens directly on your device. No cloud accounts, no tracking, and no external servers.
• Home Screen & Lock Screen Widgets: Check the latest updates at a glance.
• Encrypted Backup & Export: Export your watchlists and saved items anytime with encrypted backups and OPML support.
