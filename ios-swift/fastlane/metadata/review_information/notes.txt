OshiReader+ is retained in maintenance mode. It does not require account sign-in; its backend aggregates public feed data and delivers optional notifications.

To test the app:
1. Tap '+' to add a watch topic (for example: "乃木坂46" or "Anime").
2. Pull down on the Feed screen to refresh and fetch matching items.
3. Tap on any item to open the in-app reader.
